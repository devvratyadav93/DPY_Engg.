Execution Process

5-Stage Fabrication & Outfitting Flow

01

Drawing & Specification Review

Thorough study of naval architect blueprints, structural nestings, and isometric spool drawings.

02

Material & Consumable Verification

Traceability check of plates, pipes, and welding electrodes with Mill Test Certificates (MTC).

03

Precision Fit-Up & Tack Welding

Gap, root face, and dimensional tolerance checks prior to full penetration weld runs.

04

Class Welding & NDT Examination

Multi-pass SMAW/FCAW/GTAW welding followed by visual, DP, ultrasonic, or radiography testing.

05

Stage Sign-Off & Handover

Formal inspection presentation to Shipyard QA/QC and Classification Surveyors.
